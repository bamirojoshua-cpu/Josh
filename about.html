About Penmat Travels

Penmat Travels is a travel, mobility, and retail brand based in Turkey. We help clients with international travel, visa processing, and global relocation — and we also offer personal shopping, product sourcing, and the sale and distribution of Turkish wears and home essentials with delivery across Nigeria and worldwide.

🌐 Our Vision

Penmat Travels exists to redefine global mobility by empowering individuals, students, families, and professionals to move beyond borders with confidence, clarity, and opportunity breaking down complex immigration and travel barriers through trusted human expertise, innovative technology, and personalized global guidance that transforms international dreams into structured, achievable journeys across continents and cultures.

Meet the Founder

Founder & Travel Consultant

With years of experience helping students and professionals travel across the world, Joshua founded Penmat Travels to simplify international travel and create reliable pathways for people seeking opportunities abroad. Through dedication, industry knowledge, and a passion for global mobility, Joshua built Penmat Travels as a trusted travel consultancy focused on transparency, efficiency, and personalized support. Penmat Travels assists clients with visa processing, travel consultations, flight reservations, personal shopping in Turkey, product sourcing, and the sale of Turkish wears and home essentials. The company is committed to making global opportunities and quality products accessible by providing clear guidance and reliable solutions for people looking to study, work, travel, or shop from Turkey. With a growing network and a client-focused approach, Penmat Travels continues to support travelers at every stage of their journey from the initial consultation to successful visa approvals and departures. At its core, Penmat Travels believes that travel is more than just movement across borders; it is a gateway to new opportunities, experiences, and a better future.
