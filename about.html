Meet the Founder

Founder & Travel Consultant

With years of experience helping students and professionals travel across the world, Joshua founded Penmat Travels to simplify international travel and create reliable pathways for people seeking opportunities abroad. Through dedication, industry knowledge, and a passion for global mobility, Joshua built Penmat Travels as a trusted travel consultancy focused on transparency, efficiency, and personalized support. Penmat Travels assists clients with visa processing, travel consultations, flight reservations, and international travel guidance, helping individuals confidently navigate the often complex process of traveling abroad. The company is committed to making global opportunities accessible by providing clear guidance and reliable solutions for people looking to study, work, or explore new destinations around the world. With a growing network and a client-focused approach, Penmat Travels continues to support travelers at every stage of their journey from the initial consultation to successful visa approvals and departures. At its core, Penmat Travels believes that travel is more than just movement across borders; it is a gateway to new opportunities, experiences, and a better future.
